Source terms apply to the material they cover. Combining source values in one file does not transfer rights between its contributors.

The Ecuadorian input is `ecuador_table1.csv`. Nicaraguan inputs are `waslala_s1_numeric.csv` and `nirs_calibration.csv`. In `component_means.csv`, the study column distinguishes Colombian and Venezuelan source rows. The separate `colombia_table2_sd.csv` contains the Colombian standard deviations.
